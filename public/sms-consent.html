SMS Messaging Consent

By providing your mobile phone number to Grizzly Electrical Solutions, you consent to receive text messages related to electrical service, estimates, proposals, approvals, scheduling, job updates, and customer communication.

Types of Messages You May Receive

  • Estimate or proposal links
  • Approval or signature requests
  • Appointment and scheduling updates
  • Job status notifications
  • Follow-up communication related to your service request

Message Frequency

Message frequency may vary depending on your project, estimate, or service request.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

Opt-Out Instructions

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. After opting out, you may receive one final confirmation message.

Help Instructions

For help, reply HELP or contact Grizzly Electrical Solutions directly.

Consent is not required as a condition of purchase. You may request service or estimates without agreeing to receive SMS messages.

Privacy

We do not sell or share SMS opt-in data or consent information with third parties for their marketing purposes.
